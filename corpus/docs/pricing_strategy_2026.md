\# 2026 Pricing Strategy - Confidential



\## List Pricing

Enterprise tier list price: 180K USD ACV annual. SMB tier: 18K USD ACV. Platform fee 24K USD plus 6 USD per 1K supplier records per month.



\## Discount Authority

\- Up to 10 percent: AE discretion.

\- 10 to 15 percent: Sales Manager approval.

\- 15 to 25 percent: VP Sales plus Finance.

\- Above 25 percent: CFO approval, per the approval matrix in contract\_template\_msa.



\## Launch Pricing

The Q3 launch introduces a Faster Signals bundle at a 12 percent premium over list for the first 90 days, then reverts. This premium is baked into the 6.2M USD in new enterprise ARR assumption in finance\_forecast\_2026.



\## Competitive Positioning

We do not compete on price against legacy vendors; we compete on latency and data freshness. Any price-matching requires CFO approval.



\## Non-Standard Terms

Multi-year prepay discounts up to 8 percent are standard. Custom SLAs, data-residency clauses, and unlimited-record pricing must be approved by Legal and Finance. See contract\_template\_msa.



\## Confidentiality

Restricted to Sales, Finance, and Exec. Do not share list-price floors with customers or in public channels. The sales\_playbook\_enterprise points reps here for a reason: quote only after checking this document.
