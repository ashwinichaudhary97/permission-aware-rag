\# Executive Compensation Plan - 2026 (Restricted)



\## Philosophy

Consistent with the Employee Handbook, executive pay is performance-based. The annual salary adjustment for executives occurs each April, the same cycle as the broader company.



\## Base Salaries (2026)

\- CEO: 475,000 USD

\- CFO: 390,000 USD

\- CTO: 385,000 USD

\- CRO: 360,000 USD

\- CPO: 340,000 USD

\- VP Engineering: 310,000 USD



\## Performance-Based Bonus

Executive bonus target is 40 percent of base, with a maximum of 80 percent. Payout is gated on the 62M USD ARR plan in finance\_forecast\_2026 and on the Q3 launch hitting its 6.2M USD new-ARR commitment (see product\_roadmap\_q3). Bonus pool total: 3.4M USD (see finance\_forecast\_2026).



\## Equity

Annual refresh grants: CEO 120K RSUs, other execs 40-60K RSUs. 4-year vest, 1-year cliff.



\## Change of Control

Double-trigger acceleration on a change of control. CFO approval is required for any off-cycle equity grant, consistent with the contract\_template\_msa approval matrix.



\## Confidentiality

This document is restricted to Exec, HR Admin, and Finance. Do not reference specific figures outside those groups. The handbook public compensation language must remain the only external-facing description.
