\# Q2 2026 Customer Churn Analysis - Confidential



\## Headline

Gross churn 8.1 percent annualized, net churn 4.3 percent. SMB churn is the drag: 11.2 percent gross. Enterprise churn 4.8 percent, in line with the 8 percent gross assumption in finance\_forecast\_2026.



\## Why Customers Leave

Top reasons (exit interviews, n equals 34):

1\. Query latency: 9 accounts cited slow queries as primary. This is exactly what the Q3 launch query-latency work addresses.

2\. Missing supplier coverage in EMEA: 7 accounts.

3\. Price: 6 accounts, mostly SMB.

4\. Champion turnover: 5 accounts.



\## Account-Level Detail

\- Three enterprise accounts (above 120K USD ACV each) are at-risk flagged in CRM; two are waiting on the Q3 launch latency fix.

\- SMB churn concentrates in sub-20K USD ACV accounts with fewer than 5 seats.



\## Implications for Roadmap

The churn data reinforces prioritizing query latency reduction in product\_roadmap\_q3. Product should treat the three at-risk enterprise accounts as design partners.



\## Implications for Sales

sales\_playbook\_enterprise should lead with latency proof points for any renewal at risk. Do not discount to save SMB accounts below the floor in pricing\_strategy\_2026.

Finance, Exec, and Product only. Do not share account names outside this group.
