\# New Hire Onboarding - First 30 Days



\## Week 1: Access and Compliance

Day 1: laptop pickup, MDM enrollment, and MFA setup. Complete security awareness training within 30 days (see it\_security\_policy). Read the Employee Handbook and sign the acknowledgment. Meet your onboarding buddy.



\## Week 2: Systems and Context

Get access to the wiki, Jira, and your department shared drives. Attend the company all-hands (first Tuesday of the month). Review your team roadmap and recent meeting notes.



\## Week 3: First Deliverable

Ship one small, well-scoped task with your manager guidance. Shadow a customer call if you are in a customer-facing role.



\## Week 4: Check-In

30-day check-in with your manager and HR. Discuss role clarity, tooling gaps, and any access requests still pending.



\## Benefits Enrollment

Enroll in medical, dental, and vision within 30 days; coverage is retroactive to day one. 401(k) auto-enrollment at 3 percent with 4 percent match.



\## Questions

Ping help-it for access issues, help-hr for benefits, or your manager for anything role-specific.
