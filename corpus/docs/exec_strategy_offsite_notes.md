\## Day 2: The 2027 Bet

\## M\&A Discussion

Board (per board\_minutes\_2026\_q2) authorized the tuck-in. Offsite agreement: pursue SignalHarbor, go/no-go by end of Q3, use Project Harbor codename.



\## Restructuring Stance

Exec agreed the Q4 restructuring remains a contingency only, triggered by the downside scenario. If triggered, severance follows the handbook formula (severance is calculated at two weeks of base salary per year of service) and draws on the 1.2M USD reserve.
