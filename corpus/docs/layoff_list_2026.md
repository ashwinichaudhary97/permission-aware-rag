\# Q4 2026 Restructuring - Affected Roles (Restricted)



\## Context

Per the downside scenario in finance\_forecast\_2026, leadership has approved a Q4 restructuring affecting 38 roles (9.2 percent of headcount). The process follows the Employee Handbook RIF framework: roles identified by leadership, reviewed by Legal and HR, communicated individually.



\## Severance

Severance is calculated at two weeks of base salary per year of service, with a minimum of eight weeks, identical to the handbook public policy. Total severance cost: 1.1M USD, drawn from the 1.2M USD severance reserve in finance\_forecast\_2026.



\## Affected Groups

\- Engineering: 14 roles (mostly in the legacy ingest team post-rewrite)

\- Sales: 9 roles (SMB segment)

\- Marketing: 6 roles (field events)

\- G\&A: 5 roles (contractors converted out)

\- Product: 4 roles



\## Timeline

Notification: first week of November. Last day: mid-December. Retention bonuses for 6 critical roles are approved separately by the CEO and CFO (see exec\_comp\_2026 for the CFO approval matrix).



\## Confidentiality

This document is restricted to Exec, HR Admin, and Legal. The handbook public description of the RIF process must remain the only external-facing description. Any communication to affected employees is scripted by HR and Legal.
