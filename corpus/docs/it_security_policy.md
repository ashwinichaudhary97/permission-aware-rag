# IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy



## Scope

Applies to all Northwind employees, contractors, and interns. This policy is referenced in the Employee Handbook and violations fall under the handbook disciplinary framework.



## Device Rules

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption, MDM enrollment, and screen lock at 5 minutes. Personal devices may access email and Slack only through the approved MDM profile. USB storage is disabled by default; exceptions require manager and Security approval.



## Data Handling

Data is classified into four tiers: public, internal, confidential, and restricted. Confidential and restricted data must never be copied to personal devices or sent to external email addresses. Shared drives are access-controlled by department and role; requests to broaden access go through the data-owner review process.



## Passwords and MFA

MFA is mandatory on all company systems. Password managers are provided. Passwords must not be reused across systems.



## Incident Reporting

Report suspected incidents to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to remediate a suspected breach yourself. The incident commander will coordinate with Legal.



## Consequences

Violations of the IT Security Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination, consistent with the Employee Handbook. Repeated or willful violations are treated as gross misconduct.
