\# Data Retention and Deletion Policy



\## Scope

All Northwind data, aligned with the IT Security Policy data tiers.



\## Retention Periods

\- Customer data: retained for contract term plus 30 days, then deleted.

\- Employee records: 7 years post-termination (HR).

\- Investigation records: 7 years, restricted access (see HR investigations).

\- Financial records: 7 years.

\- Logs: 90 days hot, 1 year cold.



\## Deletion Requests

Customer deletion requests are honored within 30 days. EU customers have data-residency commitments per customer contracts; coordinate with Legal before deleting EU-resident data.



\## Legal Hold

Litigation or investigation triggers a legal hold that suspends deletion. Legal issues the hold.



\## Employee Obligations

Do not retain copies of confidential data on personal devices. This violates the Employee Handbook and IT Security Policy and can be grounds for termination.



\## Auditing

Quarterly access reviews by Security and Legal.
