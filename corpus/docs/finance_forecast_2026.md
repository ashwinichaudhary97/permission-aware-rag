# 2026 Financial Forecast - Confidential



## Summary

FY2026 plan: 62M USD ARR exiting the year, up 29 percent from FY2025. Gross margin 78 percent. Operating loss of 4.1M USD, narrowing to breakeven in Q4.



## Revenue Assumptions

The plan assumes the Q3 launch (per product_roadmap_q3) drives 6.2M USD in new enterprise ARR in Q3-Q4. SMB contributes 9.8M USD net-new; Enterprise contributes 18.4M USD net-new. Churn assumption: 8 percent gross, 4 percent net.



## Cost Structure

Headcount: 412 FTE by year-end, up from 388. Engineering is the largest cost center at 21M USD. The marketing launch budget of 180K USD is fully loaded in Q3 (per marketing_launch_plan).



## Sensitive Line Items

- Executive compensation pool: 3.4M USD, allocated per exec_comp_2026 (CFO approval required for any reallocation).

- Severance reserve: 1.2M USD, held for a potential Q4 restructuring.

- M\&A reserve: 8M USD, earmarked for a tuck-in acquisition in H2 (target not disclosed here).



## Scenarios

Base case (above), upside case (70M USD ARR), downside case (54M USD ARR). Downside triggers a Q4 restructuring and draws on the severance reserve.



## Approval

Forecast approved by CFO and CEO. Any change to revenue or headcount assumptions requires CFO sign-off.
