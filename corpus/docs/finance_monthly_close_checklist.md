# Monthly Close Checklist



## Day 1-3: Revenue

Reconcile bookings to CRM. Confirm ACV recognition matches pricing_strategy_2026 rules. Flag any multi-year prepay for ratable recognition per contract_template_msa.



## Day 4-6: Expenses

- Cloud spend: compare to vendor_negotiation_notes committed rate.

- Marketing: load actuals against launch budget.

- Headcount: reconcile to the 412 FTE plan in finance_forecast_2026.



## Day 7-8: Accruals

Accrue severance reserve per the downside scenario. Accrue exec bonus pool monthly at 1/12 of 3.4M USD.



## Day 9-10: Review

Variance analysis vs finance_forecast_2026. Any variance above 5 percent needs a written explanation to the CFO.



## Day 11: Close

Lock the period. Publish the close package to Exec.



## Notes

Never share raw close numbers outside Finance and Exec. The pricing_strategy_2026 discount floors and vendor_negotiation_notes targets are confidential inputs to this checklist.
