\# Monthly Close Checklist



\## Day 1-3: Revenue

Reconcile bookings to CRM. Confirm ACV recognition matches pricing\_strategy\_2026 rules. Flag any multi-year prepay for ratable recognition per contract\_template\_msa.



\## Day 4-6: Expenses

\- Cloud spend: compare to vendor\_negotiation\_notes committed rate.

\- Marketing: load actuals against launch budget.

\- Headcount: reconcile to the 412 FTE plan in finance\_forecast\_2026.



\## Day 7-8: Accruals

Accrue severance reserve per the downside scenario. Accrue exec bonus pool monthly at 1/12 of 3.4M USD.



\## Day 9-10: Review

Variance analysis vs finance\_forecast\_2026. Any variance above 5 percent needs a written explanation to the CFO.



\## Day 11: Close

Lock the period. Publish the close package to Exec.



\## Notes

Never share raw close numbers outside Finance and Exec. The pricing\_strategy\_2026 discount floors and vendor\_negotiation\_notes targets are confidential inputs to this checklist.
