\# HR Investigation Process Guide



\## When to Investigate

Allegations of policy violation, harassment, discrimination, or data misuse. Data-misuse cases also involve Security (see incident\_response\_plan).



\## Steps

1\. Intake: HR logs the complaint, assigns a case number.

2\. Assessment: HR plus Legal decide if a formal investigation is warranted.

3\. Evidence: preserve logs, emails, statements. Apply legal hold per data\_retention\_policy.

4\. Interviews: structured, documented.

5\. Findings: written report (see investigation\_report\_2026 for a closed example, restricted).

6\. Disposition: per the Employee Handbook disciplinary framework, up to termination.



\## Confidentiality

Case files are restricted to HR Admin and Legal. Managers are informed only on a need-to-know basis.



\## Timeline

Target 30 days from intake to disposition; complex cases may extend with Legal approval.



\## Records

Retained 7 years per data\_retention\_policy.
