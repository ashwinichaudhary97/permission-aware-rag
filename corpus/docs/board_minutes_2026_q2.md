\# Board of Directors Minutes - Q2 2026 (Restricted)



\## Attendance

CEO, CFO, CTO, CPO, CRO, four independent directors, General Counsel (non-voting).



\## 1. Financial Review

CFO presented the 2026 finance forecast (finance\_forecast\_2026): 62M USD ARR exit, 29 percent growth, 4.1M USD operating loss. Board approved the plan with a request to stress-test the downside scenario.



\## 2. Compensation

Board approved the performance-based bonus pool of 3.4M USD and the executive base salaries in exec\_comp\_2026. The annual salary adjustment cycle remains April. One director asked whether the bonus gate on the Q3 launch is too aggressive; CFO confirmed it is intentional to align execs with the 6.2M USD in new enterprise ARR target.



\## 3. M\&A

Board authorized management to pursue a tuck-in acquisition, drawing on the 8M USD M\&A reserve. Target profile is ma\_target\_profile\_2026 (restricted). Board asked for a go/no-go by end of Q3.



\## 4. Restructuring Contingency

Board reviewed the downside scenario. Management confirmed a Q4 restructuring contingency exists (layoff\_list\_2026) with a 1.2M USD severance reserve, and that severance is calculated at two weeks of base salary per year of service per the Employee Handbook. No vote taken; contingency only.



\## 5. Governance

Next board meeting: Q3, first week of October. Minutes restricted to Exec.
