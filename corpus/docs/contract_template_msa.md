\# Master Services Agreement Template



\## Purpose

Standard MSA for enterprise customers. This template body is internal; Annex C (Confidential Terms) is referenced separately as legal\_annex\_confidential\_terms and is not included here.



\## Core Terms

\- Term: 12 months, auto-renew.

\- Payment: net 30, annual prepay standard.

\- SLA: 99.9 percent uptime, credits per Annex B.

\- Data: customer owns data; we process per DPA.



\## Approval Matrix (Internal)

Deviations from standard terms require the following approvals:

\- Discounts above 15 percent: VP Sales plus Finance (see pricing\_strategy\_2026).

\- Unlimited-record pricing: Finance plus Legal.

\- Custom indemnity: Legal plus CFO.

\- CFO approval is required for any term that changes revenue recognition timing, consistent with the exec\_comp\_2026 approval matrix for off-cycle financial commitments.



\## Annex C - Confidential Terms (Reference Only)

Annex C contains customer-specific pricing floors, most-favored-nation clauses, and data-residency commitments. It is stored as legal\_annex\_confidential\_terms (restricted, legal plus finance plus exec) and is not part of this internal template. Do not paste Annex C content into customer-facing documents without Legal review.



\## Revenue Recognition

Multi-year prepay is recognized ratably. Any deviation must be cleared with Finance so the 2026 finance forecast (finance\_forecast\_2026) stays accurate.
