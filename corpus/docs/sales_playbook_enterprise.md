# Enterprise Sales Playbook



## Who We Sell To

Enterprise equals ACV above 100K USD, typically supply-chain leaders at retailers with 200M to 2B USD revenue. The Enterprise segment drives 62 percent of new bookings.



## The Faster Signals Pitch

Lead with the Q3 launch narrative: 3x ingest throughput, sub-200ms query latency, Supplier Risk Score v2. These map to the customer pain of stale supplier data.



## Discovery Questions

- How fresh is your supplier risk data today?

- What is your current p95 query latency?

- Which of your top-10 suppliers are single-sourced?



## Pricing Conversations

Never quote list price without checking pricing_strategy_2026. Enterprise discounts above 15 percent require Finance and Sales leadership sign-off per that document. Use the standard MSA from contract_template_msa; non-standard terms need Legal.



## Objection Handling

Too expensive: tie to the 6.2M USD in new enterprise ARR the launch is expected to drive company-wide, and to the customer cost of a missed supplier disruption.



## Handoff to CS

Enterprise accounts get a named CSM. Log the deal in the CRM with the launch-aligned use case so Marketing can source a case study.
