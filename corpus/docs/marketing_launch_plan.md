\# Q3 Launch - Marketing Plan



\## Objective

Drive 6.2M USD in new enterprise ARR in Q3-Q4, aligned to the 2026 finance forecast. Primary audience: supply-chain leaders at mid-market retailers (200M to 2B USD revenue).



\## Messaging

Lead with Faster Signals. Three proof points: 3x ingest throughput, sub-200ms query latency, and Supplier Risk Score v2. Position against legacy vendors on speed and data freshness.



\## Channel Plan

\- Webinar series (2 sessions, September plus October): co-hosted with two design partners.

\- Paid LinkedIn: 40K USD budget, targeted at supply-chain titles.

\- Content: 4 blog posts, 1 analyst briefing, 1 customer case study.

\- Field events: 3 regional dinners in Austin, Chicago, Dublin.



\## Timeline

Announcement: second week of September, per the Q3 product roadmap. Press embargo lifts the same day. Sales enablement session one week prior.



\## Budget

Total launch budget: 180K USD, split across paid media (40K USD), events (90K USD), content (30K USD), and agency (20K USD). This is reflected in the 2026 finance forecast marketing line.



\## Success Metrics

MQLs: 600. Pipeline influenced: 14M USD. Webinar registrations: 1,200.
