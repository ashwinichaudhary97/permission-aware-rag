# Benefits Guide 2026

## Health Insurance
Covers employee and dependents, with 80 percent premium subsidy. Three plan tiers available.

## Retirement Plan
The company matches 5 percent of employee contributions. Auto-enrollment at 3 percent with immediate vesting on match.

## Paid Time Off
15 days of annual leave, 10 days of sick leave. 11 company holidays.

## Parental Leave
16 weeks for the primary caregiver, 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Can be taken within 12 months of birth or placement.

## Wellness
75 USD per month stipend via the benefits portal. Covers gym, therapy, and wellness apps.

## Compensation Context
Bonus and salary adjustment follow the Employee Handbook schedule: performance-based bonus paid annually, annual salary adjustment each April. Specific figures are in your offer letter, not this guide.

## Note
Specific terms of the benefits plan are subject to the official insurance contract and are not covered in this guide.
