# Procurement and Vendor Management Policy



## Thresholds

- Below 10K USD: manager approval.

- 10K to 100K USD: Finance approval.

- Above 100K USD: CFO approval.

- Above 1M USD or multi-year: CFO plus CEO.



## Contract Review

All vendor contracts use the standard MSA structure from contract_template_msa where possible. Vendor paper requires Legal review.



## Cloud and Infrastructure

Cloud spend is negotiated centrally per vendor_negotiation_notes. No department may sign a separate cloud commit.



## Security Review

Any vendor touching confidential/restricted data must pass a Security review aligned with the IT Security Policy.



## Budget Alignment

All procurement must fit within finance_forecast_2026 departmental budgets. Off-budget requests escalate to CFO.



## Records

Contracts stored in the legal repository; retention per data_retention_policy.
