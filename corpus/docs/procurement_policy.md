\# Procurement and Vendor Management Policy



\## Thresholds

\- Below 10K USD: manager approval.

\- 10K to 100K USD: Finance approval.

\- Above 100K USD: CFO approval.

\- Above 1M USD or multi-year: CFO plus CEO.



\## Contract Review

All vendor contracts use the standard MSA structure from contract\_template\_msa where possible. Vendor paper requires Legal review.



\## Cloud and Infrastructure

Cloud spend is negotiated centrally per vendor\_negotiation\_notes. No department may sign a separate cloud commit.



\## Security Review

Any vendor touching confidential/restricted data must pass a Security review aligned with the IT Security Policy.



\## Budget Alignment

All procurement must fit within finance\_forecast\_2026 departmental budgets. Off-budget requests escalate to CFO.



\## Records

Contracts stored in the legal repository; retention per data\_retention\_policy.
