\# Cloud Vendor Negotiation Notes - Confidential



\## Vendor

Primary cloud provider, 3-year committed spend renewal due Q4 2026.



\## Current Spend

4.2M USD per year, growing 18 percent YoY. This is a top-3 line item in finance\_forecast\_2026 hosting costs.



\## Negotiation Goals

\- 22 percent discount on committed spend (target 3.3M USD per year).

\- Waive egress fees for EMEA data residency (supports the three EU-residency customers in legal\_annex\_confidential\_terms).

\- 99.95 percent SLA with credits.



\## Leverage

\- We are a reference customer in their supply-chain vertical.

\- Two competitors have bid.

\- Our 3-year commit is 12M USD.



\## Walk-Away

3.6M USD per year. Above that, CFO escalates to CEO.



\## Contract Terms

Use our standard MSA structure from contract\_template\_msa where possible; vendor pushes their paper. Legal flagged one indemnity clause that conflicts with our customer MFN obligations in legal\_annex\_confidential\_terms.

Round 2 scheduled. Do not share discount targets outside Finance, Legal, Exec.
