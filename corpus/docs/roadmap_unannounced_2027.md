\# 2027 Product Roadmap - Unannounced (Restricted)



\## Theme: Predictive Supply Chain

2027 bets build on the Q3 launch foundation: move from signals to predictions.



\## Initiatives

1\. Supplier disruption forecasting: ML model predicting supplier failures 30 to 60 days out. Builds on SignalHarbor scoring (see ma\_target\_profile\_2026).

2\. Autonomous reordering: API that auto-places POs when risk thresholds trigger.

3\. EMEA data residency GA: closes the gap flagged in churn analysis.

4\. Mobile app: the redesign deferred from Q3.



\## Revenue Impact

CFO finance\_forecast\_2026 assumes 2027 growth of 25 percent; these initiatives are the primary lever. Autonomous reordering is the largest ARR opportunity (12M USD target).



\## Dependencies

\- SignalHarbor acquisition must close in Q4 for the forecasting model to ship in H1 2027.

\- Hiring: 8 ML engineers.

Product and Exec only. Nothing here is announced. Do not reference in customer calls, marketing, or the product\_roadmap\_q3 internal doc. If a customer asks about 2027, say we are focused on the Q3 launch.
