# MSA Annex C - Confidential Terms (Restricted)



## Scope

This annex is the confidential companion to contract_template_msa. It contains terms that must never appear in the internal template body or in customer-facing drafts without Legal review.



## Most-Favored-Nation Clauses

Two enterprise customers hold MFN clauses guaranteeing they receive pricing no worse than any comparable customer. This constrains pricing_strategy_2026 discount floors: any deal below the MFN floor triggers a notice obligation within 10 business days.



## Data Residency

Three customers require EU-only data residency (Dublin region). This adds approximately 14K USD per year per customer in infrastructure cost, reflected in the finance_forecast_2026 hosting line.



## Custom Indemnity

One customer has a custom IP indemnity capped at 2x annual fees. Approved by CFO and Legal; do not replicate without CFO sign-off.



## Pricing Floors (per customer)

- Acme Retail: 150K USD ACV floor

- Globex Supply: 165K USD ACV floor

- Initech Logistics: 140K USD ACV floor



## Handling

Restricted to Legal, Finance, and Exec. Never forward this annex to Sales or to customers. Sales sees only the approval matrix in contract_template_msa.
