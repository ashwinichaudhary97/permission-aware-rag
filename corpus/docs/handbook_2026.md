\# Northwind Employee Handbook - 2026



\## Welcome

This handbook is the single source of truth for how we work at Northwind. It is public to every employee and updated each January.



\## Compensation Philosophy

Northwind pays for performance. Employees are eligible for a performance-based bonus paid annually, tied to individual and company goals. Annual salary adjustment occurs each April following the review cycle. Bonus targets range from 8 to 15 percent of base salary depending on level. Executive compensation follows a separate schedule maintained by the HR and Finance teams.



\## Security and Acceptable Use

Every employee must complete security awareness training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter. Violations of the IT Security Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. See it\_security\_policy for the full device, data-handling, and incident-reporting rules.



\## Restructuring and Workforce Changes

When business conditions require a reduction in force, the company follows a structured process: affected roles are identified by leadership, reviewed by Legal and HR, and communicated individually. Severance is calculated at two weeks of base salary per year of service, with a minimum of eight weeks. Details of any specific restructuring are shared only with affected employees and the leadership team.



\## Benefits

Medical, dental, and vision begin on day one. 401(k) matching up to 4 percent. 16 weeks parental leave. Wellness stipend of 75 USD per month.



\## Time Off

Flexible PTO (minimum 15 days encouraged) plus 11 company holidays. Sick leave is uncapped with manager notification.
