# Internal Investigation Report HR-2026-014 (Restricted)



## Matter

Alleged data exfiltration by a departing sales employee. Opened 2026-05-12, closed 2026-06-03.



## Findings

The employee copied a customer list to a personal USB drive on 2026-05-09, two days before resignation. This violates the IT Security Policy data-handling rules (USB storage disabled by default; confidential data must never be copied to personal devices). The Employee Handbook classifies willful violations as gross misconduct.



## Evidence

- MDM logs showing USB mount event.

- Email to personal account with attachment (customer list).

- Witness statement from manager.



## Disposition

Termination for cause, effective 2026-05-20. Legal sent a preservation letter; no litigation filed as of close. Customer list recovered and confirmed not shared externally beyond the personal email.



## Process Notes

Investigation followed the handbook disciplinary framework. HR Admin and Legal only. Do not discuss outside this group. Employee name redacted in all broader communications.



## Recommendations

- Reinforce USB-blocking in onboarding (onboarding_guide).

- Add a quarterly access review for sales roles.
